Privacy Policy

What Parlance Does

Parlance is a language writing coach that helps you practice writing in multiple languages. You write sentences and receive real-time feedback on grammar, naturalness, and word choice.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your journal entries are saved locally on your device using your browser's storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to share them. No account or sign-up is required.

Data Sent for Analysis

The web version of Parlance does not send any data to external servers for analysis. All writing stays on your device. AI-powered feedback is available in the Parlance iOS app.

No Tracking or Analytics

Parlance does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels. We do not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, or location data.

No Data Sold

We do not sell, rent, or share any of your data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Parlance uses Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) to load display typefaces. Google's privacy policy applies to font loading. No other third-party services are used.
