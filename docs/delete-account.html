Delete Your Parlance Account

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data from Parlance: Interpreter Journal (app ID com.parlance.interpreterguide).

1. Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Parlance and go to AI settings.
  2. Under your signed-in account, choose Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed, then confirm with Delete my account.

You will be asked to sign in again first. This confirms it is really you, and it is required before an account can be deleted.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you have already uninstalled Parlance or cannot sign in, email parlance.lang@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your Parlance account. Sending it from that address is what lets us confirm the account is yours.

We action these requests within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your Parlance sign-in, whether you used Apple or Google.
  • Your cloud AI usage counter.
  • Any remaining call pack balance. Unused balance is erased and is not refundable.
  • Your Parlance Plus record.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not keep a backup copy of a deleted account.

4. What is not deleted

  • Your journal entries. These are stored on your device, not on our servers, so deleting your account does not touch them. Remove them yourself from Past Entries, or delete the app.
  • Your subscription billing. Deleting your account erases our record of Parlance Plus, but it does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it separately in your Apple Account settings so it stops billing you.

5. Retention

Account data is removed as soon as the deletion runs. Sentences submitted for cloud AI analysis are not stored after the response is returned, so there is nothing further to delete. Ordinary infrastructure logs, which do not contain your journal text, roll off on our hosting provider's normal schedule.

6. Questions

See our Privacy Policy, or email parlance.lang@gmail.com.
