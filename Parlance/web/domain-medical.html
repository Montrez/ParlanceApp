Medical interpreting
Vocabulary, register, and ethics for clinical settings. Coach RAG also injects medical context when your journal sentence uses clinical keywords.
Register

Always formal with providers and patients

Core rule

Default to formal address (usted / vous / formal English). Prefer precise clinical terms over slang. Interpret everything said — nothing added, nothing omitted.

Ethics

HIPAA and role boundaries

  • Accuracy — Interpret everything said; no summarizing away content.
  • Impartiality — Do not advocate or take sides.
  • Confidentiality — All information is confidential (HIPAA).
  • Role boundaries — You are not a cultural broker, advisor, or clinician.
  • Sight translation — Read the full document first, then translate maintaining register.
