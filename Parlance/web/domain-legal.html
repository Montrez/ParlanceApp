Protocol

First person, precise terms

Core rule

Interpret in the first person as the speaker. Use precise legal terms. Request clarification through the judge when needed. Do not summarize rights language.

Rights

Miranda / caution language

  • Right to remain silent / derecho a permanecer en silencio / droit de garder le silence
  • Right to an attorney / derecho a un abogado / droit à un avocat
  • Presumption of innocence / presunción de inocencia / présomption d’innocence
  • Render rights verbatim when the source is a fixed formula.
